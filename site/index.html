The acceleration plane

One deliberate layer.
A lot less waiting.

Super Lightning only proxies public GET and HEAD routes you explicitly configure. It caches safely, compresses intelligently, collapses cache stampedes, and keeps serving known-good responses when an origin has a bad minute.

Your requests remain in your network. The data plane does not call Magnexis, Stripe, or an analytics service.

01

Cache once.
Serve many.

Coalesces concurrent identical requests, honors ETags, and delivers Brotli or gzip from local memory.

02

Keep momentum
through failures.

Stale-on-error, stale-while-revalidate, compatible-origin fallback, and circuit breakers protect your origin.

03

Tune with
evidence.

A local Lightning Console, Prometheus metrics, route warmup, and acceleration analysis make the gains observable.

A REAL CONTROL PLANE

Move from a faster page to a faster system.

Super Lightning never routes arbitrary destinations. It accelerates only declared public reads, exposes every cache decision, and gives operators enough evidence to tune safely.

01

Build a route contract

Explicit prefixes, upstreams, safe query normalization, cache lifetime limits, response bounds, and rate controls.

02

Protect the origin

Request coalescing, stale-while-revalidate, stale-on-error, compatible backups, and circuit breaking are all active data-plane behavior.

03

Read the acceleration

Open the local Lightning Console, inspect the acceleration brief, warm public routes, or scrape Prometheus metrics.

04

Ship without lock-in

Node 20, Docker, a local configuration file, and no paid control plane required for the accelerator itself.
